mirror writing is nearly always undertaken with the left hand, and left-handers, and those whose languages are written leftwards, have an unusual facility for this writing. firstly, the various circumstances in which mirror writing occurs are surveyed, then possible underlying processes are considered and finally it is proposed that mirror writing is a topic that warrants further study, and some suggestions for such studies are outlined. after commenting on mirrored motor and visual engrams, the possibility that the right hemisphere may play an important part is entertained, and leonardo da vinci’s unique, habitual mirror writing proves to be of unexpected relevance. the reasons why left-handed writing is mirrored, and the factors that tend to inhibit mirroring, are outlined. further investigations, ranging from epidemiological to functional imaging studies, may provide valuable insights into mirror writing correspondence to: national hospital for neurology and neurosurgery, queen square, london wc1n 3bg, uk; this writing is seen in healthy individuals; it is also associated with various focal lesions that most commonly involve the left hemisphere, as well as with certain diffuse cerebral disorders. this review considers mirror writing from the neurological perspective. the reasons why left-handed writing is mirrored, and the factors that tend to inhibit mirroring, are outlined. motor pathways that may be important, the surrogate model of bimanual mirror movements and the contribution of the corpus callosum are then discussed. after commenting on mirrored motor and visual engrams, the possibility that the right hemisphere may play an important part is entertained, and leonardo da vinci’s unique, habitual mirror writing proves to be of unexpected relevance. mirror writing is nearly always undertaken with the left hand, and left-handers, and those whose languages are written leftwards, have an unusual facility for this writing. review mirror writing: neurological reflections on an unusual phenomenon g d schott  j neurol neurosurg psychiatry 2007;78:5–13. the reasons why left-handed writing is mirrored, and the factors that tend to inhibit mirroring, are outlined. review mirror writing: neurological reflections on an unusual phenomenon g d schott  j neurol neurosurg psychiatry 2007;78:5–13. mirror writing is nearly always undertaken with the left hand, and left-handers, and those whose languages are written leftwards, have an unusual facility for this writing. review mirror writing: neurological reflections on an unusual phenomenon g d schott  j neurol neurosurg psychiatry 2007;78:5–13.
