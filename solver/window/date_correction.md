# Correction: the article is Fall 2021, and the Nov-2021 peel fits best

## What I got wrong

Earlier in this session I told the user the article "shipped by February 2022",
on the strength of tweet 1492173067723886594 (2022-02-11) showing Bukele
holding the printed El Salvador edition. I then used that to re-rank the peel
candidates, saying `1AkNdBrf` at 27 Feb 2022 sat "16 days after publication,
not in third place", and that the user's Nov-2021 "magazine-on-sale week"
label was "anchored 13 months late".

That was wrong. A photograph of someone holding a magazine is an **upper**
bound on its publication, not the publication date. Bukele was reading a
months-old issue.

## The actual date

**Bitcoin Magazine Issue 24 = "The El Salvador Issue", Fall 2021.** Confirmed
by independent retail listings (eBay, Amazon, Lazada, The Crypto Merchant), and
matching the parallel session's finding on branch
`claude/keiser-overdose-puzzle-itkf6t`, which also has internal evidence from
the text itself:

- "Stacy and I have been living in here for **10 years**" → 2021
- "ADA, and **12,000** other shitcoins" → that coin count is late 2021
- Issue 27 = "The Orange Party Issue", Fall 2022, is a *different* issue

So the correction against the March-2023 tweet everyone was anchoring on is
about **18 months**, not 13.
